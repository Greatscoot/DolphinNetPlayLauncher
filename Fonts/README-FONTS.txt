Dolphin NetPlay Launcher - Fonts

Outfit is bundled with Dolphin NetPlay Launcher and is used by the launcher interface.

Included files:
  Outfit-Regular.ttf
  Outfit-Bold.ttf

Outfit is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
See OFL-Outfit.txt for the full license.

Assistant-generated chat-development packages may omit the TTF binaries even though the OFL permits redistribution. That omission is an artifact-delivery restriction, not a Dolphin NetPlay Launcher licensing choice. Normal public release packages should include Outfit-Regular.ttf, Outfit-Bold.ttf, and this OFL notice.
